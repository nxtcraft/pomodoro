Pomodoro Technique — Complete Guide to Deep Focus

The Pomodoro Technique, developed by Francesco Cirillo in the 1980s, is one of the most studied and effective time management methods. It uses a simple principle: work in short, focused bursts with built-in breaks. Here's everything you need to know.

Why It Works

Your brain can sustain deep focus for only 20–30 minutes before attention drifts. The Pomodoro Technique works with your brain's natural attention cycle rather than against it. The mandatory short break prevents mental fatigue from building up, while the fixed time creates urgency that fights procrastination.

Step-by-Step: The Classic Pomodoro Cycle

Step 1 — Define ONE taskBefore starting the timer, write down the specific task you will work on. "Study for exam" is too vague. "Solve Chapter 5 integration problems" gives your brain a clear target.
Step 2 — Start the 25-minute timerClick Start. Close all unrelated tabs. Put your phone face-down. Commit fully to the task until the timer rings. If a stray thought appears, write it down quickly and return to focus.
Step 3 — Take the 5-minute break (mandatory)When the bell rings, stop — even if you're in flow. Stand up, walk, hydrate. The break is not optional. Skipping it defeats the method.
Step 4 — After 4 Pomodoros, take a long breakAfter completing 4 sessions (~2 hours of focused work), reward yourself with a 15–30 minute break. Go for a walk, eat a snack, rest your eyes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if 25 minutes is too long for me?
Start with 15-minute sessions. Gradually increase to 25 minutes as your focus improves. The timer is adjustable in our tool.
Can I use Pomodoro for work, not just studying?
Yes. Pomodoro works for any focused task — writing reports, coding, responding to emails, or creative work. Many software engineers use it for deep coding sessions.
What if I finish the task before the timer ends?
Use the remaining time to review your work, improve it, or prepare for the next session. Never start a new task mid-Pomodoro.
Does the browser tab need to stay open?
The timer uses browser notifications, so it works even if you switch tabs. Enable notifications when prompted.
